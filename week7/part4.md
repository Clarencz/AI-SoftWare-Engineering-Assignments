## Part 4: Ethical Reflection (5%)

**Project:** "ChamaWealthHub" (Fintech App for Savings Groups)

**Reflection:**
To ensure ChamaWealthHub adheres to ethical AI principles, I must address the risk of **financial exclusion**. If we implement AI for credit scoring within the Chamas:
1.  **Fairness:** I will ensure the algorithm does not penalize users based on location or lack of traditional banking history (proxy bias), instead focusing on internal cash-flow data.
2.  **Transparency:** Users will see exactly which factors contribute to their credit limit.
3.  **Privacy:** Individual financial data must be strictly siloed; AI insights for the group must be aggregated to prevent exposing an individual member's financial struggles to the whole group.
